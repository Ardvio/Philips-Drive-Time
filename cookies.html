Cookie Notice

This Cookie Notice explains how Philip's Drive Time uses cookies when you use our platform.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit, such as whether you are logged in.

Cookies We Use

Philip's Drive Time uses essential cookies only. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies of any kind.

Essential

Authentication token

Set by Supabase to keep you securely logged in during your session. Expires when you log out or after your session ends.

Session identifier

Used to maintain your secure connection to the platform. Deleted when you close your browser.

These cookies are strictly necessary. The platform cannot function without them and they cannot be disabled without logging out of the service entirely.

Cookies We Do Not Use

  • Google Analytics or any analytics cookies
  • Facebook Pixel or any advertising cookies
  • Third-party tracking or retargeting cookies
  • Social media cookies

Your Choices

Because we only use essential cookies, no cookie consent banner is required — essential cookies do not require consent under UK PECR.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, blocking essential cookies will prevent you from logging in to Philip's Drive Time.
