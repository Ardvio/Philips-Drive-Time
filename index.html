Questions

Good to know.

How do I book my first lesson?

Send Philip a message on Facebook. He'll get back to you to talk through where you're at and find a time that suits. Once you're a pupil, you can book lessons straight from your learner portal.

Where are lessons?

In and around Enniskillen, Co. Fermanagh. Ask Philip about pick-up when you get in touch.

When can I have lessons?

Monday to Saturday. Weekday and Saturday lessons are both available.

Do you teach manual or automatic?

Lessons are manual, in a dual-controlled Volkswagen Golf.

I'm really nervous. Is that okay?

Completely. Lots of pupils start out nervous. Philip keeps things calm and builds up step by step, so you're never pushed into something you're not ready for.

Can my parents see how I'm getting on?

Yes. From your learner portal you can create a read-only progress link to share with family.
