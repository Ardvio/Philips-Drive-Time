1. The Service

Philip's Drive Time is a digital portal for managing driving lessons between Philip Hughes (ADI) and registered pupils. The platform provides:

  • Online lesson booking and diary management
  • Driving skill progress tracking (NI DVA syllabus)
  • Lesson notes and history
  • Secure messaging between pupil and instructor
  • Optional parent/guardian progress sharing

The platform is built and maintained by Ardvio. Philip Hughes is responsible for the delivery of all driving instruction.

2. Lesson Pricing

Lesson rates are set by Philip Hughes and communicated directly to each pupil prior to booking. Prices are subject to change with at least 14 days' notice.

3. Booking & Confirmation

Lessons can be booked through the Philip's Drive Time portal. A booking is confirmed once it appears in your dashboard. Philip reserves the right to decline a booking request at his discretion.

4. Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellations made more than 12 hours before the lesson: no charge
  • Cancellations made less than 12 hours before the lesson: full lesson fee applies
  • No-shows without any notice: full lesson fee applies

To cancel, use the portal or contact Philip directly. Verbal or WhatsApp cancellations are accepted but must be made within the 12-hour window to avoid a charge.

5. Instructor Cancellations

Philip reserves the right to cancel or reschedule a lesson at any time. In the event of an instructor-initiated cancellation:

  • No charge will apply to the pupil
  • Philip will make reasonable effort to offer an alternative slot
  • In exceptional circumstances (illness, emergency, adverse weather), cancellations will be made as early as possible
